Inside FormWaypoint

Two shipping workflows

Standard shipping

Trace quantities, net weights and values back to the invoice and packing list. Review the Schedule B commodity rows before generating a Shipper’s Letter of Instruction for Nippon Express or CEVA, or a keying sheet for FedEx or UPS.

Dangerous goods by air

Describe each battery type and how it is packed. FormWaypoint checks the package details, identifies missing answers, and prepares the package checklist and Shipper’s Declaration where required.
